How I analyse data

      
Every project answers a business question with a repeatable process. If a number appears on this page, you can trace it back to a dataset and a repo.

      

        
1. Question
Define the decision the data should support.

        
2. Source
Name the dataset, its origin and its limits.

        
3. Clean
Handle nulls, duplicates and outliers; log each fix.

        
4. Analyse
SQL and Python to test the question, not just describe data.

        
5. Insight
State the finding in one plain sentence with a number.

        
6. Action
Recommend what the business should do next.

Questions clients ask

      

        
What do I need to give you?
A short brief: the decision you want to make, the data you have (even if it is messy) and your deadline.

        
Is my data safe?
The health check on this page runs in your browser and never uploads your file. For real projects we agree how data is shared and stored before I open it.

        
How do I know the numbers are right?
Every project records its source, cleaning steps and queries so you can re-run them. My GitHub shows the work and its history.

        
Which tools do you use?
SQL, Python, Google Sheets, Looker Studio, Power BI and Excel, chosen to match what your team already uses.
